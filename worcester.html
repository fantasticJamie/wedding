Located on Foregate Street right next to the train station. It features hotel rooms, an indoor pool, and an on-site bar and brasserie.

Premier Inn Worcester City Centre

Located on New Road next to the cricket ground. It offers reliable, straightforward rooms and an on-site restaurant.
