Where to Stay

We'll be staying at Hotel Torre del Mar (Playa d'en Bossa) ourselves and would love for as many of you as possible to join us in the same hotel. We singled it out during our visit in May and were shown around. It looks like an awesome hotel (zero pressure here though!)

There are also a ton of other options and this location was chosen so we have easy access to Ibiza Town for Friday night and also Nassau Beach Club on Sunday. We've been told that they expect to be very busy during our period and to book soon. They are currently offering decent early discounts but rates will increase.
